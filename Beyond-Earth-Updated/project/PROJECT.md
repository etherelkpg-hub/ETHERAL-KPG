# Project brief

## Problem

Choosing a field site for an early Mars equipment trial requires comparing a specific property, such as loose soil or frozen ground, with the conditions the instrument needs to handle. An Earth site can be useful for one test and a weak match for another.

## Approach

Players choose a destination, use region-specific equipment, compare observations with Mars references and review results in their research journal.

| Destination | Example investigation |
| --- | --- |
| Atacama Desert | Dry sediment and mineral signatures |
| Haughton Crater | Rover mobility and rough impact terrain |
| Death Valley | Salt flats, dunes and terrain comparisons |
| Wadi Rum | Surface sampling and rover navigation |
| McMurdo Dry Valleys | Ice indicators and cold-weather observations |

## Judge walkthrough

1. Choose **Start Game**, then follow the mission in the top-left corner. Use **WASD** or the arrows to move, **Shift** to run and **E** to interact or advance dialogue.
2. Follow the city’s **AIRPORT →** sign. Choose a destination at a ticket kiosk, then board at the indicated gate.
3. At a field site, open **I** to inspect the equipment. The highlighted cards match the tools in the central ring.
4. Press **E** at a tool, choose a setup and run the simulation. Use **J** for trial outcomes and the sample collector’s **Field Records** for imported observations.
5. Open the **Comparison Terminal** southeast of the ring and choose a property. Choose **Overall comparison** to see the site's average rating across its rated properties. Entries marked **Not assessed** are excluded from the average.
6. Press **M** for the exploration map. Walk to the southern exit and press **E** to return to Denver Airport.

The four redesigned maps bring real landforms into a small, walkable area. The in-game paths and relative positions are adapted for exploration.
